The [integration walkthrough in protocol-doc](https://github.com/aragon/protocol-doc/blob/main/guides/use-ve-governance.md) covers deposits, delegation, gauge votes, result reads, and withdrawals with Solidity examples.

## Gauge result accounting

The [gauge voting reference](https://github.com/aragon/protocol-doc/blob/main/plugins/ve-governance/gauge-voting.md) describes both accounting modes, storage buckets, hook-driven reductions, and reads at a fixed block. The walkthrough shows how to turn those reads into allocations.
