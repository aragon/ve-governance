= Aragon VE Governance

Aragon VE Governance provides vote-escrow and gauge-voting contracts for onchain organizations. Token holders deposit an ERC-20 token, receive an NFT representing the position, and use voting power to assign weights to registered gauges.

Protocols can use the resulting weights to allocate incentives or other resources. The VE contracts account for positions, delegation, votes, and withdrawals. Reward calculation, funding, and payout require a separate integration.

== The participant's workflow

. Approve the underlying token and create a lock in the escrow.
. Receive a position NFT. The curve determines its voting power from its amount and recorded start.
. Delegate to your own address or another participant through the IVotes adapter.
. Assign relative weights to active gauges during the voting window.
. Begin withdrawal when the minimum lock requirement is met, then withdraw according to the exit ticket's waiting period and fee. A pending withdrawal can also be cancelled.

For a new integration, start by evaluating v1.4, the configuration described in these guides. Earlier-version documentation supports existing deployments and their upgrade paths; Season is a separate variant.

The v1.4 configuration combines a linear curve with address-based voting, delegation, and an exit queue that supports several fee models. Its gauge allocations persist across epochs and can decrease when delegated power falls. Choose a snapshot block when using those results for rewards.

== Components

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Component |Responsibility
|Voting escrow |Holds the underlying tokens and manages positions and withdrawals.
|Lock NFT |Represents ownership of each position and enforces transfer permissions.
|Curve |Calculates position and total voting power from checkpointed history.
|IVotes adapter |Aggregates delegated voting power by address and exposes timestamp-based queries.
|Gauge voter |Records participants' weighted preferences across registered addresses.
|Clock |Defines epochs, checkpoint intervals, and the voting window.
|Exit queue |Records withdrawal tickets, eligibility, and fees.
|OSx DAO and plugins |Control administration, permissions, and upgrades.
|===

Your protocol supplies proposal execution, reward payouts, and the emission schedule through separate governance and distribution components.
